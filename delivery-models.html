How can I work with DISTILLOGIC?

Choose complete project ownership, a defined work package, dedicated engineering capacity or an ongoing delivery partnership.

4 delivery structures for different programme needs.

01

Project-Based Delivery

Defined outcomes with clear ownership.

Full Project Delivery

We take responsibility for an entire project from planning through production, with agreed scope, milestones, deliverables and acceptance criteria.

Work Package Delivery

A defined part, module or technical stream within a larger programme is assigned to DISTILLOGIC.

Application Module Delivery

We deliver a specific application component such as a portal, reporting module, API layer or authentication module.

Fixed Scope / Fixed Price

A specific result, price, milestones, deliverables and acceptance criteria are agreed before delivery begins.

02

Team-Based Delivery

Engineering capacity aligned with your roadmap.

Dedicated Engineering Team

A stable DISTILLOGIC team works against the client roadmap and within the client technology environment.

Engineering Squad

A focused cross-functional team supports a specific product, stream or delivery objective.

Staff Augmentation

Individual engineers join and work as part of the client team.

Nearshore Team

DISTILLOGIC operates as a European engineering delivery centre for the client.

Time & Material

The client purchases engineering capacity based on time, roles and effort.

03

Continuous Delivery

Ongoing ownership for live applications and services.

Application Management

Continuous maintenance, enhancement, releases, bug resolution and technical support for an existing application.

Managed Services

Ongoing operation and support for an agreed application or technology service.

Continuous Enhancement

Backlog delivery and incremental improvement throughout the application lifecycle.

Application Support

L1, L2 and L3 support, incident resolution, production investigation, releases and upgrades.

04

Strategic Delivery Partnerships

Flexible participation in larger programmes and tenders.

Subcontracting

A lead systems integrator holds the main contract and DISTILLOGIC delivers an agreed part of the project.

White-Label Delivery

We deliver on behalf of another company without appearing to the end client, where permitted by contract.

Tender Subcontracting

DISTILLOGIC participates as a subcontractor from the tender stage.

Framework / Call-Off Delivery

Delivery takes place through a framework agreement and individual call-off assignments.

Consortium / Delivery Partnership

We work with other organisations to support a larger project or tender.

Build–Operate–Transfer

We build the solution, operate it for an agreed period and transfer it to the client.
